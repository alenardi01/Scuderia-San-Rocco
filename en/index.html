Horse riding in Chianti

Scuderia San Rocco is part of my small farm, surrounded by the rolling hills of Chianti. Here I produce a little wine, olive oil and lavender, and I share this peaceful and authentic landscape with my horses. It’s a simple and natural place, where riding becomes a way to truly experience the countryside, slowly and in harmony with the animals.
