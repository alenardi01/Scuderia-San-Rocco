Horse riding in Chianti

Scuderia San Rocco is part of my small farm, surrounded by the hills of Chianti. Here I produce a bit of wine, olive oil and lavender, and I share this authentic and peaceful landscape with my horses. It’s a simple and natural place, where riding becomes a way to experience the territory slowly, following the rhythm of the animals and the countryside.
